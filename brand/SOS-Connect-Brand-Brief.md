# SOS Connect — Brand Brief

## 1. Brand Name

**SOS Connect**

### Meaning

**SOS** represents a need for assistance.

**Connect** represents the solution: connecting people who need help with people willing to provide it.

SOS Connect is a community-based platform designed to make it easier for people to ask for help, find help, and provide help.

A person providing assistance through the platform is called a **Connector**.

---

## 2. Brand Positioning

SOS Connect is a community-based platform that connects people who need help with people willing to give it.

Unlike platforms that simply direct people toward organizations, businesses, or existing services, SOS Connect focuses on creating direct connections between people within their communities.

SOS Connect is designed to accommodate many different types of needs and support, from everyday assistance to transportation, care, sharing resources, and emergency recovery.

### What Makes SOS Connect Different

SOS Connect is built around the belief that help should work both ways.

Someone can receive help today and become the person providing help tomorrow.

**Help Goes Both Ways.**

---

## 3. Mission

SOS Connect exists to make it easier for people to find and provide meaningful help within their communities, creating a connected network where anyone can ask for support, offer a helping hand, and pay it forward.

### Short Mission

**Connect people. Give help. Pay it forward.**

---

## 4. Vision

### Primary Vision

**A world where no one feels like they have to face a problem alone.**

### Alternate Expression

**A world where help is never too far away.**

---

## 5. Core Values

### Compassion

Treat people with kindness, empathy, and understanding.

### Community

Build stronger communities by encouraging people to support one another.

### Connection

Create meaningful connections between people who need help and people who can provide it.

### Trust

Create an environment where people feel safe, respected, and comfortable asking for or providing help.

### Pay It Forward

Encourage people who receive help to give help when they are able.

### Accessibility

Make help easier to find and provide regardless of a person's circumstances.

---

## 6. Target Audience

SOS Connect is designed for anyone who needs help and anyone willing to provide it.

### People Who Need Help

Examples include:

- Older adults
- Busy parents
- People without nearby support
- People experiencing temporary difficulties
- People who need transportation
- People who need assistance around their home
- People looking for resources
- People recovering from emergencies or disasters

### Connectors

Connectors may include people who:

- Want to give back
- Have useful skills
- Have available time
- Have resources they can share
- Want to support their community
- Want to help someone nearby

---

## 7. Brand Story

SOS Connect was inspired by a lifetime of seeing what it means to help others.

The founder was raised by a mother whose life centered around helping people. She adopted children, advocated for children and people in need, gave food to people experiencing homelessness, volunteered through churches and an animal shelter, worked with the NAACP, supported family members, and consistently found ways to help others.

That example created a lasting belief that helping people is more than an occasional act — it can become a way of life.

The founder also experienced the importance of receiving help personally. Friends, family, and community services provided support during some of life's most difficult moments as well as some of its best.

Those experiences created a desire to pay that support forward.

SOS Connect was created around the idea that the help someone receives today can become the help they give tomorrow.

The goal is to build a platform that can connect people anywhere who need assistance, regardless of the type of help they need.

**Receive help. Give help. Pay it forward.**

## 10. Tagline

**Help Is Closer Than You Think.**

---

## 11. Verbal System

SOS Connect uses three core ideas:

### NEED

People should feel comfortable asking for help.

Examples:

- "Need a hand?"
- "Tell us what you need."
- "You're not alone."

### CONNECT

SOS Connect helps people find someone nearby.

Examples:

- "Let's find someone nearby."
- "Connect with someone who can help."
- "Find a Connector."

### GIVE

People can provide help to others.

Examples:

- "Become a Connector."
- "Have a skill or resource to share?"
- "Give help when you can."

---

## 12. Big Brand Idea

# Help Goes Both Ways.

SOS Connect is not only about receiving help.

It is about creating a community where people can:

**Need help → Receive help → Give help → Pay it forward**
