ABOUT SOS CONNECT

Help Goes Both Ways.

SOS Connect was built around a simple belief: the help someone receives today can become the help they give tomorrow.

Our Story

It started with a way of life.

SOS Connect was inspired by a lifetime of seeing what it means to help others.

“Helping people is more than an occasional act. It can become a way of life.”

I was raised by a mother whose life centered around helping people.

She adopted children, advocated for children and people in need, gave food to people experiencing homelessness, volunteered through churches and an animal shelter, worked with the NAACP, supported family members, and consistently found ways to help others.

Watching her showed me that helping people does not have to be something you do once in a while. It can become part of who you are.

But I also learned the other side of helping: receiving it.

Friends, family, and community services provided support during some of life's most difficult moments as well as some of its best.

Those experiences created a desire to pay that support forward.

SOS Connect was created around that idea.

The help someone receives today can become the help they give tomorrow.

The goal is to build a platform that can connect people anywhere who need assistance, regardless of the type of help they need.

What We Believe

Nobody should feel like they have to face a problem alone.

Sometimes you need someone to help you. Sometimes you are the person who can help. SOS Connect brings those two moments together.
